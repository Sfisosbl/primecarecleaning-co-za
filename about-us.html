About PrimeCare Cleaning

We clean with care because people live, work and serve in these spaces.

PrimeCare Cleaning is a South African cleaning company built around two kinds of empowerment: dignified work for women cleaning professionals and real relief for households, teams and institutions carrying the burden of keeping spaces healthy.

Our purpose

Care for clients. Dignity for cleaners. Standards buyers can trust.

We are building PrimeCare around respectful work, trained teams, clear checklists and reliable supervision. The goal is not just a clean room. The goal is a service relationship that feels professional, human and dependable.

The business is designed to serve both sides of the cleaning equation: clients who need dependable relief and cleaning professionals who deserve to be seen, supported and developed.

01

Women-powered teams

We prioritise work pathways, training and leadership opportunities for women in cleaning.

02

Client relief

We help families, caregivers, professionals and facility managers remove cleaning stress from the week.

03

Professional systems

Every service is shaped by checklists, arrival windows, access notes, product preferences and reporting.

Our values

How PrimeCare should feel.

Respectful

Reliable

Ready to grow

PrimeCare is being structured for households today and formal contracts with serious buyers tomorrow.

How we work

Clear communication before, during and after the clean.

Before

We collect the service type, site notes, access requirements, product preferences and priority areas.

During

The team follows the agreed priorities, works carefully through high-touch areas and escalates anything unusual.

After

PrimeCare can follow up for recurring schedules, contract discussions, facility needs or service feedback.
